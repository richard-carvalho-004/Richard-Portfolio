About Me

DevOps Engineer | CI/CD & Automation | Cloud & Observability

I build and maintain the pipelines, environments and monitoring that let product teams ship with confidence. At EG A/S I work across CI/CD, cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes and observability, keeping delivery fast and failures visible.

Recently I've migrated 150+ Jenkins pipelines to GitHub Actions with Octopus Deploy, automated Kubernetes config and secrets delivery with FluxCD, and built Grafana dashboards that turn Checkmarx scan results and VM health into something teams actually look at.

I enjoy replacing manual, fragile processes with automation, and I use AI-assisted tooling where it helps, with every change reviewed before it merges. I started out as a data analyst, which is why I care about dashboards that answer real questions.
